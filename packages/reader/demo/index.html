Reading is a skill we build once and use all day

Research finds that no single font or setting is best for everyone. In one large study, readers were about 35% faster in their own best font than in their slowest, and their favorite font was also their fastest only one time in five.

So this page lets you change what matters: the font, the size, the spacing between lines, letters, words and paragraphs, how wide the column is, and the colors. Press Alt + A or the Aa button to open the panel. Every change is held inside a safe range, and when a color pair would be hard to read, the text color is corrected for you and you are told.

Try the Dyslexia-friendly preset, then Night, then push the line length to its maximum. Notice that the line height rises on its own once the column is wider than seventy-five characters, because long lines need more room for the eye to find the start of the next one.

Your choices stay on this device. Save them as your default, copy a link to share them, or export them as a file.
